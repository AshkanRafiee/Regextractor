Regextractor turns highlighted examples into a regular expression.

Paste or type any sample text, highlight the parts you want to capture, and the app infers a pattern that matches them. Multiple highlights are generalized: shared structure collapses into one pattern with count ranges, while different shapes become a clean alternation.

Features:
- Infer regex from one or many highlighted examples
- Smart generalization of letters, digits, whitespace and literal characters, including case-aware letter classes
- Ready to use out of the box: matches land in group 1 (named groups optional), so extraction works right after pasting
- Copy-ready output forms: plain pattern, JavaScript literal or quoted Java/Kotlin string literal
- Live preview listing every match in your text; tap a match to copy it
- Toggle case-insensitive, multiline, dot-matches-all and find-all behavior
- One-tap sample presets (log lines, dates, emails, prices, IDs)
- Share any text into the app from other apps to extract from it
- Copy or share the finished pattern

Private by design: Regextractor works fully offline, declares no permissions, and never sends your text anywhere. No account, no analytics, no internet access.
